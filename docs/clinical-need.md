# Clinical Need

Patients recovering from lower-limb injuries and orthopedic procedures are often prescribed partial weight-bearing restrictions.

Current monitoring frequently depends on verbal instruction, patient self-reporting, and intermittent clinician observation. These methods do not provide continuous objective measurements or immediate feedback when a patient exceeds a prescribed load.

The project addresses the need for an affordable, wearable system that measures bilateral plantar loading and communicates the results to patients and rehabilitation clinicians in real time.
